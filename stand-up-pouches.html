Custom Stand Up Pouches

Stand-up pouches are the most widely used flexible format for retail food, coffee, snacks and powders. BOYUE customizes the structure, closure, window and finish around your product, filling method and shelf life.

Available Bag Types

Bag Formats We Produce

  • Standard Stand-up Pouch
  • Stand-up Pouch with Zipper
  • Stand-up Pouch with Window
  • Stand-up Pouch with Valve
  • Stand-up Pouch with Handle

Every format is customized to your pack weight, filling method and shelf-life requirement. Specifications and structures are confirmed before production.

Materials & Options

Material Structures and Features

Structures are selected according to barrier needs, shelf life, filling method and destination market requirements.

Available Materials

  • PET / PE — standard barrier for dry snacks and short shelf life
  • PET / VMPET / PE — metallized barrier for aroma and light protection
  • PET / AL / PE — high barrier for oxygen- and moisture-sensitive products
  • MOPP / PE — matte, premium exterior finish
  • Kraft paper / PE — paper-forward eco retail look
  • PLA / PBAT or paper-based structures — biodegradable and recyclable options on request

Available Features

  • Resealable Zipper
  • Tear Notch
  • Clear or Shaped Window
  • One-Way Valve
  • Spout & Cap
  • Bottom Gusset for Shelf Standing
  • Laser Scoring
  • Handle or Punch Hole
