Process

From Enquiry to Quotation

No account setup and no form to fill in twice. Send the brief once and the questions come back in one message.

What happens after you send the form

  1. We read the brief. Your bag type, size, material, quantity and artwork status are checked, and anything missing is asked for in one message rather than several.
  2. We propose a structure. A film structure, thickness and accessory set is recommended against your shelf life and filling method, with the alternative routes shown when there is more than one.
  3. You get an indicative price. Price is quoted against the confirmed specification, quantity and printing method, so it can be compared directly with another supplier's offer.
  4. Sample or proof. Where the project moves forward, a printed proof or physical sample is arranged before production so the structure can be checked in the hand.

One person handles the enquiry from the first message through to the export documents, so artwork, structure questions and inspection stay on the same thread.

Information that speeds up the quote

  • Product to be packed, and whether it is food, pet food or non-food
  • Bag format and fill weight per pack
  • Material structure if you already know it, or the shelf life you need
  • Quantity per design and number of designs
  • Printing method preference, or leave it to us to recommend
  • Artwork files, or a note that design is still open
  • Destination country and any labelling or compliance requirement
  • Preferred shipping term — EXW, FOB, CIF or DDP
Questions

Common Questions Before You Write

Short answers to what new buyers ask most often.

How quickly will I get a reply?

Enquiries are answered from Qingdao, normally within one business day. Messages that arrive overnight in China are handled at the start of the next working day.

Can I get a price without full specifications?

An indicative range is possible from bag type, fill weight and quantity, but a comparable price needs the structure, thickness and printing method. Sending the full brief first saves a round of questions.

Do you send samples before production?

Printed proofs or physical samples can be arranged once the specification is agreed, so the film feel, print colour and closure can be checked before the run.

Which shipping terms do you work with?

EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP are all discussed per order. The term is confirmed together with the destination port and the documents required for that market.
