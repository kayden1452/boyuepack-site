Manufacturing & Production Process

The processes and manufacturing resources available through Boyue and its selected manufacturing partners in China, from digital and gravure printing to finished pouch making and warehouse storage.

The factory and process images on this page are illustrative references representing the manufacturing resources available through Boyue and its manufacturing partners. They are not presented as photographs of Boyue's own facilities.
Core Advantage · Short Runs

HP Indigo 200K Digital Printing

The HP Indigo 200K digital press is a core competitive advantage for short-run, multi-design flexible packaging. It allows small batches and several designs to be produced without traditional gravure cylinders.

Purpose: New product launches, market testing, seasonal packaging, sample bags and small SKU batches.

  • No gravure plates or cylinders for digital jobs
  • Multiple designs or SKUs in one order
  • Fast artwork revisions and shorter turnaround
  • Suitable for hundreds to a few thousand pieces
High Volume · Bulk Orders

Gravure Printing

Traditional rotary gravure printing for large-volume repeat orders, where unit cost and long-run color stability matter most.

Purpose: Established products and repeat bulk shipments that benefit from a lower per-unit price.

  • Lower unit cost at high volume
  • Stable, consistent color across long runs
  • Vivid, durable printed layers
  • Commonly suited to orders above roughly 10,000 pieces
Material Structure

Lamination

Two or more film layers are bonded together to build the barrier and mechanical structure required for the product.

Purpose: Combining barrier, print, seal and strength functions in a single flexible web.

  • Custom barrier against moisture, oxygen and light
  • Different film functions combined in one structure
  • Structure matched to product shelf life and regulations
Bond Stabilization

Curing

Laminated rolls are held under controlled conditions to stabilize the adhesive bond before slitting and pouch making.

Purpose: Ensuring the layers stay bonded through downstream processing and the product's shelf life.

  • Stable adhesive bonding after lamination
  • Reduces the risk of delamination
  • Consistent performance in later production stages
Width Preparation

Slitting

The laminated film web is slit into the widths specified for the bag size or the customer's automatic packaging machine.

Purpose: Preparing film rolls that fit pouch making equipment or production lines.

  • Width matched to bag size or machine requirement
  • Tight tolerance on roll width
  • Clean edges for reliable sealing
Finished Bags

Pouch Making

Film is formed, sealed and cut into finished bag styles, with features such as zippers, spouts, valves, windows and handles added as required.

Purpose: Producing ready-to-fill stand-up pouches, flat-bottom bags, spout pouches, side gusset bags and other formats.

  • Multiple bag styles and formats
  • Zipper, spout, valve, window and handle options
  • Finished bags prepared for filling and packing
Process Control

Quality Inspection

Print registration, sealing, dimensions and finish are checked across incoming material, in-process production and finished goods.

Purpose: Catching defects early and confirming the finished bags meet the agreed specification before packing.

  • Print and seal quality checked before packing
  • Defects identified at the earliest possible stage
  • Documentation and test reports available on request
Material & Finished Goods

Warehouse & Storage

Raw film rolls, auxiliary materials and finished pouches are kept in a controlled warehouse, organized by material structure, batch and order before dispatch.

Purpose: Keeping materials and finished goods organized so orders can be packed and shipped on schedule.

  • Raw film and material rolls stored by structure and batch
  • Finished bags held before packing and shipment
  • Batch tracking for repeat orders and follow-up
