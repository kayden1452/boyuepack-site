Flexible Packaging Manufacturing Capabilities

Digital Printing & Gravure Printing

BOYUE works with professional flexible packaging manufacturing partners equipped with digital printing, gravure printing, lamination and pouch-making capabilities. By matching different production methods to different order requirements, we provide flexible solutions for both short-run and large-volume packaging projects.

The factory and process images on this page are illustrative references representing the manufacturing resources available through Boyue and its manufacturing partners. They are not presented as photographs of Boyue's own facilities.
Core Advantage · Short Runs

Digital Printing – HP Indigo 200K

Our HP Indigo 200K digital printing resources are ideal for short-run, multi-design flexible packaging, where small batches and several artwork versions have to be produced without traditional gravure cylinders.

Ideal for:

  • Small-batch packaging orders
  • Multiple designs and SKUs in one order
  • New product launches
  • Market testing and sample bags
  • Short-run custom packaging

Key advantages:

  • No traditional gravure plate or cylinder costs
  • Flexible artwork changes before printing
  • Suitable for multiple artwork versions
  • Efficient short-run production and faster turnaround
  • High-quality digital print output

Digital printing gives brands more flexibility when testing new products or managing multiple packaging designs.

High Volume · Bulk Orders

Gravure Printing

For larger production volumes, our manufacturing partners provide gravure printing solutions for customized flexible packaging. Traditional rotary gravure is used where unit cost and long-run color stability matter most.

Suitable applications:

  • Large-volume packaging orders
  • Long-term production programmes
  • Multiple-layer flexible packaging
  • Food and pet food packaging
  • Repeat bulk shipments above roughly 10,000 pieces

Gravure printing is suitable for projects requiring consistent print reproduction and efficient large-scale production.

Printing Method Comparison

Digital Printing vs Gravure Printing

The right route depends on your order quantity, number of designs and how often the artwork changes.

Digital Printing

Suitable for small and medium runs.

  • Ideal for multiple designs
  • No traditional gravure plate costs
  • Flexible artwork changes
  • Suitable for new product launches
  • Better fit for market testing and sample runs

Gravure Printing

Suitable for large-volume orders.

  • Ideal for long-term production
  • Requires gravure cylinders
  • Suitable for repeat production
  • Efficient for large-scale orders
  • Lower unit cost once volume is established
Material Structure

Lamination

Two or more film layers are bonded together to build the barrier and mechanical structure required for the product.

Purpose: Combining barrier, print, seal and strength functions in a single flexible web.

  • Custom barrier against moisture, oxygen and light
  • Different film functions combined in one structure
  • Structure matched to product shelf life and regulations
Bond Stabilization

Curing

Laminated rolls are held under controlled conditions to stabilize the adhesive bond before pouch making.

Purpose: Ensuring the layers stay bonded through downstream processing and the product's shelf life.

  • Stable adhesive bonding after lamination
  • Reduces the risk of delamination
  • Consistent performance in later production stages
Finished Bags

Pouch Making

Film is formed, sealed and cut into finished bag styles, with features such as zippers, spouts, valves, windows and handles added as required.

Purpose: Producing ready-to-fill stand-up pouches, flat-bottom bags, spout pouches, side gusset bags and other formats.

  • Multiple bag styles and formats
  • Zipper, spout, valve, window and handle options
  • Finished bags prepared for filling and packing
Process Control

Quality Inspection

Print registration, sealing, dimensions and finish are checked across incoming material, in-process production and finished goods.

Purpose: Catching defects early and confirming the finished bags meet the agreed specification before packing.

  • Print and seal quality checked before packing
  • Defects identified at the earliest possible stage
  • Documentation and test reports available on request
Material & Finished Goods

Warehouse & Storage

Raw film rolls, auxiliary materials and finished pouches are kept in a controlled warehouse, organized by material structure, batch and order before dispatch.

Purpose: Keeping materials and finished goods organized so orders can be packed and shipped on schedule.

  • Raw film and material rolls stored by structure and batch
  • Finished bags held before packing and shipment
  • Batch tracking for repeat orders and follow-up
