About Boyue Packaging

A flexible packaging supplier based in Qingdao, China, focused on food-grade bags for export markets — from material selection to finished, export-ready packaging.

Who We Are

A One-Stop Packaging Channel From China

QINGDAO BOYUE TRADE CO., LTD. supplies custom flexible packaging to brands, distributors and importers around the world. We work with selected packaging manufacturers to offer a full range of bags — from stand-up pouches and mylar bags to feed and fertilizer packaging.

We handle material selection, artwork, sampling, production follow-up and export documentation, so buyers get a reliable single contact for the whole packaging process.

Whether you need a trial order with digital printing or a high-volume production run with gravure printing, we match the bag structure and printing method to your budget, product and shelf-life requirements.

  • Material and structure advice for your specific product
  • Sample bag approved before mass production
  • Production follow-up and quality checks at each stage
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and export documents prepared
  • Responsive support by email and WhatsApp
  • Both sea and air shipment options available

What We Do

🧪

Material & Structure Advice

Recommend film structures (PET, ALU, PE, MOPP, kraft, PLA/PBAT) based on content, barrier needs and target market regulations.

🎨

Printing & Bag Making

Digital printing for small quantities; gravure printing for large orders. Zipper, spout, valve and other features available.

🚢

Export Documentation

Prepare commercial invoice, packing list, PI and export paperwork to support customs clearance and shipping.

Our Working Process

A transparent four-step flow, with sample confirmation before bulk production.

1

Inquiry & Specs

Share bag type, content, size, material, quantity and market. We advise on structure and printing method.

2

Sampling

Artwork and structure confirmed, sample bag produced and sent for your approval.

3

Production

Printing, lamination and bag-making, followed by inspection and export packing.

4

Shipping & Docs

Sea or air shipment arranged, with full export documents ready for customs clearance.
