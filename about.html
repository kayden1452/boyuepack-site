About Us

A flexible packaging supplier based in Qingdao, China, focused on food-grade bags for export markets.

Who We Are

QINGDAO BOYUE TRADE CO., LTD. supplies custom flexible packaging to brands, distributors and importers around the world. We work with selected packaging manufacturers to offer a full range of bags — from stand-up pouches and mylar bags to feed and fertilizer packaging.

We handle material selection, artwork, sampling, production follow-up and export documentation, so buyers get a reliable one-stop packaging supply channel from China.

Whether you need a trial order with digital printing or a high-volume production run with gravure printing, we match the bag structure and printing method to your budget and shelf-life requirements.

What We Do

Material & Structure Advice

Recommend suitable film structures (PET, ALU, PE, MOPP, kraft, PLA/PBAT) based on content, barrier needs and target market regulations.

Printing & Bag Making

Digital printing for small quantities; gravure printing for large orders. Zipper, spout, valve and other features available.

Export Documentation

Prepare commercial invoice, packing list, PI and export paperwork to support customs clearance and shipping.
