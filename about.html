Company Overview

Who We Are

Qingdao Boyue Trading Co., Ltd. is a flexible packaging supplier based in Qingdao, China. We work with professional flexible packaging manufacturing partners to provide customized packaging solutions for global customers.

Our capabilities cover digital printing, gravure printing, laminated flexible packaging and various customized pouch structures.

With access to HP Indigo 200K digital printing technology and traditional gravure printing resources, we support both short-run, multi-design projects and large-volume packaging orders.

  • Material and structure advice matched to your product
  • Sample confirmation before bulk production
  • Production follow-up and quality checks at each stage
  • Export documentation prepared for customs clearance
  • Responsive support by email and WhatsApp

Our Manufacturing Network

Working With Selected Manufacturing Partners

Boyue operates as a packaging supplier and trading partner. We coordinate material selection, artwork, sampling, production follow-up and quality checks with professional flexible packaging manufacturing partners in China.
