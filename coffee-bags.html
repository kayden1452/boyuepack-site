Materials & Options

Material Structures and Features

Structures are selected according to barrier needs, shelf life, filling method and destination market requirements.

Available Materials

  • PET / PE — standard barrier for short shelf life
  • PET / VMPET / PE — metallized barrier, cost-effective aroma protection
  • PET / AL / PE — high barrier for long shelf life and oxygen sensitivity
  • Kraft Paper Laminated Structures — paper-forward retail look
  • Customized High-Barrier Materials — structure confirmed per product and market

Available Features

  • One-Way Degassing Valve
  • Resealable Zipper
  • Tear Notch
  • Matte or Glossy Finish
  • Custom Printing
  • Tin Tie (where applicable)

Printing Options

Digital Printing or Gravure Printing

Digital printing is the practical route for new roasts, multiple origins or seasonal designs without cylinder cost. Gravure printing is used once volumes are established and a stable design repeats.
