Why Digital Printing?

HP Indigo 200K digital printing is well suited to new product launches, market testing and small-batch SKUs.

  • Short-run production — small quantities from hundreds to a few thousand pieces are workable.
  • Multiple designs in one order — different SKUs or versions can be produced together.
  • No traditional gravure printing plates for digital jobs, avoiding cylinder setup cost.
  • Flexible artwork changes — revisions can be made quickly before printing.
  • Suitable for new product launches and market testing before committing to large bulk production.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from packaging buyers. MOQ and lead time depend on product specifications, printing method and order quantity.

What is your minimum order quantity?
MOQ depends on the bag type, material structure, printing method and order quantity. Digital printing can accommodate smaller runs, while gravure printing is more suitable for larger volumes. Please share your specifications and quantity, and we will advise a workable option.
What is the difference between digital and gravure printing?
Digital printing does not require gravure cylinders and suits short runs, multiple designs and quick artwork changes. Gravure printing uses engraved cylinders and provides a lower unit cost and stable color for large repeat orders. The best route depends on your quantity and design.
Can I customize my own artwork?
Yes. All packaging products can be customized with your own brand logo, artwork, colors, sizes and packaging design. We can review your files and advise on printing and structural suitability.
Do you provide samples?
Sample bags can be arranged to confirm structure, printing and finish before bulk production. Sample availability and conditions are discussed case by case based on your requirements.
What materials are available?
We work with structures such as PET, PE, ALU/foil, MOPP, RCPP, kraft paper and biodegradable options like PLA/PBAT. The structure is selected according to barrier needs, grease resistance, shelf life and destination market requirements.
Can you produce food-contact packaging?
Food-contact material options are available for edible products. Food-contact documentation and test reports can be provided on request according to the specific product and market.
What is the typical production lead time?
Lead time varies with product specifications, printing method, order quantity and sample confirmation. Digital jobs are generally faster to start; gravure bulk orders take longer due to cylinder and production setup. We confirm the schedule after your specifications are locked.
Can you handle multiple designs in one order?
Digital printing is more flexible for combining multiple designs or SKUs in one order. For gravure bulk orders, each design normally requires its own cylinder. We can discuss the most cost-effective combination for your range.
