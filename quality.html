Quality & Certificates

Quality management covers incoming materials, production stages and finished goods. Documentation and test reports are provided on request according to the specific product and destination market.

How We Manage Quality

Quality checks follow the packaging from raw film through printing, lamination and finished bags. Test reports and compliance documents are arranged case by case.

📋

Quality Management System

Specifications are agreed before production, and each stage is followed up against the confirmed bag structure, size, artwork and finish.

🧪

Raw Material Inspection

Films, foils, zippers, spouts and other incoming materials are checked for grade, barrier and appearance before production starts.

🎨

Printing Quality Control

Color registration, print clarity and artwork consistency are checked during digital or gravure printing.

🧱

Lamination Quality Control

Bonding quality and layer structure are monitored to confirm the required barrier and seal performance.

🔍

Finished Product Inspection

Sealing, dimensions, zipper/spout function, finish and appearance are inspected on finished bags before packing.

📏

Packaging Performance Testing

Relevant performance checks such as seal strength, barrier and leak tests can be arranged according to the product and its processing conditions.

🥫

Food Contact Material Testing

Food-contact material options are selected for edible products; food-contact test reports can be provided on request for the specific material and market.

📑

Compliance Documentation

Documents are prepared according to the destination market. Available documentation and reports are confirmed per product rather than assumed in advance.
