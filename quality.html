Quality & Compliance

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Reliable Quality Control for Every Packaging Project.

From material selection and printing to lamination, pouch making and final inspection, we focus on maintaining consistent quality throughout every stage of production.

Production Stage Control

Quality Control Process

Quality checks follow the packaging from incoming film through printing, lamination and pouch making to finished goods. Each stage is followed up against the confirmed bag structure, size, artwork and finish.

01

Raw Material Inspection

Films, foils, zippers, spouts and other incoming materials are checked for grade, barrier and appearance before production starts.

  • Incoming film, foil and auxiliary materials
  • Material grade and barrier confirmation
  • Appearance check before release to production
02

Printing Quality Control

Color registration, print clarity and artwork consistency are checked during digital or gravure printing.

  • Color registration during the print run
  • Print clarity and ink consistency
  • Artwork checked against the approved design
03

Lamination Quality Control

Bonding quality and layer structure are monitored to confirm the required barrier and seal performance.

  • Bonding quality of the laminated layers
  • Layer structure as specified for the bag
  • Barrier and seal performance confirmation
04

Pouch Making Quality Control

Sealing, cutting and finishing are monitored as film is formed into bags, with zippers, spouts, valves, windows and handles added as required.

  • Bag style and dimensions against specification
  • Sealing and closure function (zipper, spout, valve)
  • Cutting, edges and overall finishing
05

Finished Product Inspection

Sealing, dimensions, zipper/spout function, finish and appearance are inspected on finished bags before packing.

  • Seal and dimension checks on finished bags
  • Zipper and spout function verification
  • Final appearance before packing
Seal · Barrier · Leak Testing

Packaging Performance Testing

Relevant performance checks such as seal strength, barrier and leak tests can be arranged according to the product and its processing conditions.

  • Checks selected according to product and process
  • Seal, barrier and leak related verification
  • Reports arranged case by case
